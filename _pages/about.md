Hi! I am Ananya Bandopadhyay, a Physics PhD candidate and NASA FINESST Fellow at Syracuse University working with [Eric Coughlin](https://ecoughli.expressions.syr.edu/). My research is focused on understanding the fate of stars on orbiting close to supermassive black holes, which can get ripped apart in a tidal disruption event (TDE). I use analytical and numerical methods to model astrophysical transients that these destructive encounters give rise to, particularly ones that repeat on timescales of a few months to years. You can read more about my research on my [Research](/research/) page.
