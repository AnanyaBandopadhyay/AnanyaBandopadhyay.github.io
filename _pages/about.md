Hi! I am Ananya Bandopadhyay, a Physics PhD candidate and NASA FINESST Fellow at Syracuse University working with [Eric Coughlin](https://ecoughli.expressions.syr.edu/). My research is focused on understanding the fate of stars orbiting close to supermassive black holes, which can get ripped apart in a tidal disruption event (TDE). I use analytical and numerical methods to model astrophysical transients that these destructive encounters give rise to, particularly ones that repeat on timescales of a few months to years. I have also worked on projects focused at understanding the interior composition of neutron stars, and the nature of strongly interacting nuclear matter, by studying the gravitational wave signals emitted by binary neutron star systems. You can read more about my research on my [Research](/research/) page.
